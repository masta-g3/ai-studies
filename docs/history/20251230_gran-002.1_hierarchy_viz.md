# gran-002.1: Hierarchy Visualization

**Completed**: 2025-12-30
**Epic**: Concept Granularity

## Summary

Interactive D3.js visualization of academic paper hierarchy data from gran-002. Implements an indented tree with quantitative bars following Tufte's principles.

## Design Decisions

### Why Indented Tree + Bars

| Criterion | Indented Tree | Sunburst | Treemap |
|-----------|--------------|----------|---------|
| Data-ink ratio | **Excellent** | Moderate | Good |
| Level comparison | **Excellent** | Poor (angles) | Poor (nesting) |
| Scalability | **Excellent** | Moderate | Moderate |
| Direct labeling | **Yes** | Limited | Limited |

Key benefits:
- Text IS the data (category names display directly)
- Aligned bars enable immediate magnitude comparison
- Whitespace encodes hierarchy without ink
- Collapse/expand reveals detail on demand

### Visual Spec

- **Font**: IBM Plex Mono (technical aesthetic)
- **Color**: Single blue accent (#2563eb) for bars, gray structure
- **Layout**: Wide label area (580px), 20px padding before bars
- **Sorting**: Descending by paper_count at every level
